Privacy Policy for Town Bounce

Developer: Haeoreum Lab
Effective date: October 13, 2026
Contact: haeoreumlabkr@gmail.com

Haeoreum Lab provides the mobile game Town Bounce (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Account and game progress

You can play the App without creating an account or signing in. The App uses local device storage (including SharedPreferences) to save game-related settings and progress, such as stage information, inventory, and preferences. This information is stored on your device for gameplay functionality. We have not identified Firebase integration in the reviewed project configuration.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The App integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, as applicable to your device, settings, consent choices, and the SDK configuration. These data may be used to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising, including personalized advertising where permitted.

For information on Google's data practices, see Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob guidance.

3. Purposes of processing

Local game information is used to maintain game progress and preferences. Advertising-related information processed by Google or its partners may be used to display ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain service reliability.

4. Data sharing

Advertising-related data may be transmitted to and processed by Google and its advertising partners through the AdMob SDK. Haeoreum Lab does not require you to submit account registration information to play the App.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Locally stored game data generally remains on your device until it is cleared through device/app settings or the App is uninstalled, subject to device backup and restore behavior. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's applicable policies and settings. For questions or requests relating to information under our control, contact us at the email below.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not require account registration. Advertising data handling and any applicable age-related protections depend on the App's audience configuration, ad request settings, and applicable law. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us. We will review and respond to privacy-related concerns.

7. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to protect information within our control. Third-party advertising services may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable law.

8. Your choices

You may manage advertising privacy choices through your device settings and any consent options presented in the App, where available. You may remove locally stored game information by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it, subject to device backup behavior.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, advertising services, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email haeoreumlabkr@gmail.com.
